Photographs where you can see the story. Artists pin details in their work and write what's behind each one. Viewers either walk through the pins in order or tap them in any order.
TRY
1. Tap Walk through on a post. Tap the right side of the photo to go forward and the left side to go back.
2. Switch to Explore to tap the details in any order.
3. Open the replies on a detail and add one.
4. Tap + to annotate a photo and publish it to your portfolio.
The photos are placeholders. Send real images and I'll use them.
