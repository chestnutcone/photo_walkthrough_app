## Retention (placeholder)
Drafts expire after 24 hours and data exports after 7 days. Sign-in codes and sign-in attempt records are deleted after 24 hours. Account data is kept while the account exists. After you delete your account it is removed within minutes, or, if some of your content is under moderator review, when that review ends and at most 30 days after your request; database backups and our storage provider's recovery copies keep deleted data for up to 7 more days before it is destroyed. The following records are kept after deletion: evidence of illegal content (see below); copyright (DMCA) notices and counter-notices about your stories, which contain the claimant's details and, for a counter-notice, your statement, kept as legal records for up to 3 years after the notice is closed; our moderation audit trail (which action a moderator took on your account or content, when and why); and, if your account was banned, a one-way hash of each sign-in identity so the ban cannot be evaded by signing up again. If you delete your account while some of your content is under moderator review, a one-way hash of each sign-in identity (with the date of your request and the date it lapses, nothing else) stops the same sign-in from creating a new account until that review ends, at most 30 days after your request; the hash is deleted when the block lapses.
